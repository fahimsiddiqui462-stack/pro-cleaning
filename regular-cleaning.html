PRO CLEANING / CARE IN EVERY DETAIL

Choose a realistic frequency

Busy homes and workplaces may benefit from weekly attention. Quieter spaces may suit a less frequent schedule. Match the plan to use, not appearances.

Know the difference

A maintenance clean focuses on routine tasks. A deep clean includes more detailed work that should be specified in the quote.

Agree on priorities

List the rooms and surfaces that matter most. Discuss pet areas, sensitive finishes, and any products you prefer before the visit.

Review the plan

Your needs can change over time. Check the scope occasionally so the service stays useful and fits your schedule.
