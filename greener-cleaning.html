A more thoughtful approach to greener cleaning

Practical home-care notes · 4 minute read

PRO CLEANING / CARE IN EVERY DETAIL

Start with less

Use only the amount recommended on the label. More product does not automatically mean a better clean, and can leave residue.

Choose for the surface

A mild detergent and a reusable cloth are often enough for everyday dirt. Check product instructions and surface compatibility first.

Keep safety simple

Never mix cleaning chemicals, especially bleach with ammonia or acids. Ventilate the room and store products away from children and pets.

Build reusable habits

Wash reusable cloths regularly, separate bathroom and kitchen cloths, and replace disposable items where practical.
